What does Geopolitica mean?

Geo is the Earth: its places, borders, sea lanes and resources. Politica is the affairs of states and the people in them. Together they ask one question: how does geography shape who holds power, and how they use it?

Geopolitica follows that one event at a time. A strike in one country, a war at sea or a vote in a capital ripples into prices, jobs and lives far away. The map shows how, and why, in a few minutes.

Free for anyone learning geopolitics. Every event links to its sources; connections carry the sentence that justifies them.
